Connected devices

    Ambient settings

    For the bridge's own ambient look (not a music program). Changes show straight away and are kept for next time.

    Tick lights to control them. A light belongs to whoever ticks it first, until they untick it or close the page; lights nobody has ticked are dark, unless ambient is on.

    Solo

    The soloed light flashes white; every other moving light is dark. Click a light's name below, or use ↑ and ↓, to pick another.

    Each kind of light has its own controls below, and only shows what that kind can do. Controls are greyed out until you tick a light of that kind.

    Channel finder

    Poke any single DMX channel to find out what it does. Overrides everything else. Starts at the dimmer of the first light.
